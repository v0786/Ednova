## Project purpose

Create EDNOVA as a secure, auditable, multi-tenant Digital Academic Operating System for Schools using specification-first, phase-gated delivery.

## Objectives

- Establish a complete engineering documentation system.
- Define and validate Phase 1 foundation requirements before coding.
- Deliver the seven governed phases only through their documented gates.
- Preserve student history and protect tenant and student data.
- Maintain traceability from business need through release evidence.

## Scope authority

The governing master prompt is the initial product and engineering source of truth. Stakeholders, product owner, approvers, budget authority, jurisdiction, service-level commitments, and vendor contracts are TBD.

## Deliverables

Governance baseline, requirements, discovery, architecture, database design, API contracts, UX flows, module specifications, development standards, QA artifacts, release/operations/maintenance documents, implementation, tests, phase sign-offs, and implementation reports.

## Constraints

- No application feature implementation during bootstrap.
- No fabricated requirements, approvals, metrics, tests, or production status.
- No architecture or schema change without documentation.
- Only one development phase may be active.

## Success criteria

The product, governance, acceptance, security, privacy, performance, budget, adoption, and operational measures are TBD pending stakeholder validation.
