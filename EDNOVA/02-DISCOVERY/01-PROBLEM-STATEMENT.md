## Problem

Schools need an integrated academic operating system that connects structure, teaching, learning, practice, assignments, assessment, learning gaps, revision, progress, parents, and school management. Without connected records and controlled relationships, academic workflows and history can become fragmented.

## Known evidence

The problem statement is derived from the EDNOVA master prompt. No interviews, workflow observations, school data, baseline metrics, or user research artifacts were found in the repository. The severity, frequency, current alternatives, and quantified impact are TBD.

## Target outcome

Enable a secure, multi-tenant, auditable system where:

- administrators establish academic structure and identities;
- teachers operate within assigned classes and subjects;
- students and parents see authorized learning and progress;
- attendance, assignments, assessment, gaps, and revision remain connected;
- historical records are preserved.
